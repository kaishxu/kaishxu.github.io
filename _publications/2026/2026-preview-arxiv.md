abstract: >-
  We propose PreView, an auxiliary preprocessor that improves the inputs to a frozen GUI grounding model without additional training. PreView prepares task-relevant views for localization and candidate-specific visual and textual cues for target understanding, enabling grounding models to locate small controls in cluttered screenshots more accurately.
